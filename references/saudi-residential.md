# Saudi villa planning guidance

Use this reference as a design checklist, not as a substitute for permit review. Project-specific setbacks, coverage, height, parking, fire, accessibility, utility, and civil-defence requirements vary by municipality, plot, occupancy, and authority. Verify the current Saudi Building Code and the active Ministry of Municipalities and Housing residential-building requirements before claiming compliance.

## Residential zoning logic

### Guest zone

- Place the guest entrance near the majlis or formal reception space.
- Give the guest WC a controlled route from the guest lobby.
- Avoid a direct visual or walking path from the guest entrance into the family living room, kitchen, bedroom corridor, or stair serving private bedrooms.
- Use a lobby, screened turn, controlled door, or separate corridor as a privacy buffer.

### Family zone

- Place the daily family entrance near parking and the family living spaces.
- Keep bedrooms and family lounges away from guest arrival and service traffic.
- Provide a practical route from family living to dining, kitchen, outdoor family space, and the vertical core.
- Prevent bedroom doors from opening directly into formal guest spaces.

### Service zone

- Connect service parking or the service gate to kitchen, pantry, laundry, maid room, waste handling, and service storage with a short route.
- Avoid carrying groceries, laundry, waste, or maintenance equipment through the majlis or primary family seating area.
- Provide a service entrance where the site and project brief permit it.
- Align wet rooms, shafts, kitchen plumbing walls, laundry, and mechanical extract routes vertically where practical.

## Core coordination

- Align stair, elevator, and service shafts across all floors.
- Keep the elevator landing visible and accessible from the main circulation path without opening directly into a private bedroom.
- Reserve headroom and landing space at every stair flight.
- Keep doors, wardrobes, furniture, and plumbing fixtures outside stair and elevator clear zones.
- Coordinate roof access, parapets, water tanks, solar equipment, and service penetrations before freezing the upper-floor layout.

## Room and furniture checks

- Test every bedroom with the intended bed size, bedside tables, wardrobe depth, door swing, and clear walking routes.
- Test living and majlis rooms with seating depth, coffee tables, media wall, serving route, and door paths.
- Test dining with occupied-chair clearances and a direct serving path from the kitchen.
- Test kitchens with counters, appliances, islands, work aisles, pantry access, ventilation, and service entry.
- Test bathrooms with fixture clearances, wet/dry separation, door swing, drainage route, and ventilation.
- Add linen, pantry, cleaning, luggage, seasonal, under-stair, outdoor, and general storage based on the household brief.

## External planning

- Check turning, gate width, parking bay geometry, pedestrian safety, and the route from parked vehicles to family and service entrances.
- Separate pedestrian and vehicle conflict points where the plot allows.
- Coordinate refuse collection, gas, electrical, water, drainage, air-conditioning equipment, and maintenance access.
- Mark north and review solar exposure, privacy from neighbours, street views, and usable shaded outdoor areas.

## Validation interpretation

- An `error` means the generated geometry is internally inconsistent or conflicts with a configured hard threshold.
- A `warning` means the arrangement needs architectural review or a project-specific decision.
- A clean validator result does not certify authority approval, structural design, fire safety, accessibility, MEP design, or construction documentation.
